Our story

สุโข: ease and happiness

Sukho takes its name from the Thai word for happiness. We cook the food people crave on a busy street: hot woks, bright herbs and sauces with real depth. The room stays calm and welcoming, so a quick takeaway feels as good as a long dinner with family.
